- **THE ATLAS IS NOT DROP-IN, despite the grid matching.** SRI24's grid is exactly 240×240×155 at 1 mm as the plan said, but it is **anterior–posterior mirrored** relative to BraTS voxel indexing (brain-mask Dice 0.7334 as-is vs 0.9394 flipped), and `pbmap_*` is additionally **left–right mirrored relative to every other file in the same distribution**. - **THE HEADLINE, 2026-08-16:** `neurovision` trains and beats the baseline on accuracy — **Dice ET +0.0267 (p_holm 1.4e-21), TC +0.0103**, a 17.1% relative cut in ET error. But **the reliability claim is dead**: calibration, boundary accuracy AND MC-dropout risk-coverage were all measured against a matched baseline and all came back **within noise**. The project's stated claim was "reliability, not raw accuracy"; the data says the opposite. Full numbers and the paired statistics: `docs/experiments.md` notes 11–17. Do not write the reliability framing.
- **THE COST CLAIM IS REFUTED FOR DISAGREEMENT AND TRUE FOR ENTROPY, 2026-08-23.** Per-voxel MC-dropout mutual-information maps were generated for both external cohorts on the T4 (N=10; SSA 1.25 h, PED 2.07 h) and all three signals scored at the SAME sampled voxels. Mean per-case AUROC for per-voxel error — **SSA: entropy 0.8874, disagreement 0.7095, MC 0.8661. PED: entropy 0.8307, disagreement 0.7879, MC 0.8437.** Paired TOST at the margin of **0.03 fixed in `execution_plan.md` Phase 2 before any external MC map existed**: disagreement is **NOT equivalent** to MC on either cohort (SSA −0.1566, PED −0.0558, intervals entirely OUTSIDE the margin on the wrong side — not underpowered nulls), and it beats MC in only **30 of 159** cases. **Entropy IS equivalent to MC on both** (SSA +0.0214 p 0.0238; PED −0.0129 p 3.7e-05) — simultaneously significantly different and practically equivalent, which is what a margin is for. **So "disagreement matches MC-dropout at 1/10 the cost" is dead; what is true is that free single-pass entropy matches 10-sample MC-dropout, i.e. MC's tenfold cost buys nothing here — a finding about the BASELINE, not about this architecture.** Delete the efficiency framing Gate 1's *Partial* row licensed. `docs/experiments.md` note 39.
- **THE P2 NULL FIRED, 2026-08-23: THE AMBIGUITY CONDITIONING IS NOT WHAT PRODUCES THE GAIN.** `ablation_content_only_gate` (the full model with `model.fusion.use_ambiguity: false`, parameter-matched to 0.018%, same tree `7caacfa`, same seed, 80/80 epochs, 24.16 GPU-h) scores test **ET 0.8686 / TC 0.9158 / WT 0.9333** and is **INCONCLUSIVE against `neurovision` on every metric** — ET +0.0022 (CI −0.0067 to +0.0152, p_holm 0.17), TC +0.0003, WT −0.0012, all three HD95 rows likewise. It nonetheless beats `baseline_unet3d` by **+0.0244 ET** (p_holm 3.9e-22) and `capacity_control` by **+0.0189** (p_holm 4.4e-20). **Full decomposition of the +0.0267 ET gain: width +0.0055, content-only gated fusion +0.0189, ambiguity conditioning +0.0022 (indistinguishable from zero).** So ~92% of the architectural gain is the gated fusion itself, and the project's claimed novelty contributes nothing measurable. `docs/research/contribution.md` P2 pre-registered this outcome and requires it be reported. **Do not write that the disagreement signal produces the accuracy gain.** Caveats that must travel with it: the CI admits an effect up to +0.0152 so this is "no detectable difference at n=189", NOT proven equivalence (that needs a TOST with a pre-set margin); single seed, no noise floor; and the ablation's `best.pt` is its FINAL epoch 79 while `neurovision` peaked at 69, so the ablation was still improving — which handicaps it and makes the null more robust. Evaluated on the GPU deliberately, matching `neurovision`'s own eval device, because the effect is small enough for CPU-vs-GPU float differences to matter. `docs/experiments.md` note 38.
- **GATE 2 IS DECIDED, 2026-08-23: PARTIAL BY THE RULE, NEGATIVE IN SUBSTANCE.** A two-feature combiner (per-case rank-transformed entropy + disagreement, logistic, fitted ONCE on the 187-case val split and applied frozen) does **not** localise per-voxel error better than entropy alone. In distribution BOTH endpoints get significantly worse (AUROC −0.0013 p_holm 0.042; recall@5% −0.0394 p_holm 0.0006); SSA is unchanged on AUROC and worse on recall (−0.0128); **PED alone improves, on AUROC only, +0.0121** (CI +0.0102 to +0.0139) with no operational gain. 4 of 6 Holm rejections, but two are LOSSES, and `passed_cohorts` is empty. **This does not contradict Gate 1 — the gap between them IS the finding: disagreement carries error information entropy lacks (Gate 1) and that information still fails to improve a fitted operating detector (Gate 2). Incremental information is not incremental utility.** Known limitation of the gate's own design, recorded rather than fixed post hoc: the combiner is fitted by pooled likelihood while the endpoints are per-case rank metrics, and a rank-optimised refit after seeing these numbers would be exactly what pre-registration prevents. `docs/experiments.md` note 37, `docs/research/preregistration_gate2.md` §Result.
- **GATE 1 IS DECIDED, 2026-08-23, AND IT IS THE FIRST NON-NEGATIVE RESULT IN A WHILE — BUT ONLY HALF OF IT.** Verdict **PARTIAL** against `docs/research/preregistration_ambiguity.md`, over all 348 cases (test 189 / SSA 60 / PED 99), family run **once** on complete data, **zero GPU hours**. Voxel level (residualised AUROC for per-voxel error, incremental over entropy): **test 0.578, SSA 0.569, PED 0.677**, all p_holm 0.0025 with CIs excluding 0.5, and **ET is the strongest region on every cohort — 0.733 / 0.689 / 0.784**. Case level (partial Spearman vs Dice, controlling for entropy): **−0.393 in distribution** (p_holm 0.0006) but **−0.173 on SSA and +0.000 on PED, both CIs containing zero**. So: **inter-branch disagreement says WHERE the model is wrong, beyond entropy, and keeps saying it under distribution shift; it does NOT say WHICH CASE will be bad.** The mechanism is visible in the table — externally, entropy ALONE already predicts case quality strongly (ρ −0.782 SSA, −0.554 PED), leaving no case-level headroom. **Do not write "better than entropy at flagging bad cases"** — the external data refutes it. Two cases (`BraTS-SSA-00215-000`, `BraTS-PED-00051-000`) have an empty predicted WT mask, so they drop from the case-level analysis (n 59/98) but fall back to whole-volume sampling at voxel level (n 60/99) — two different denominators, never reported as one. `docs/experiments.md` note 35. **Consequence: Gate 2 must be respecified before it runs** — referral is a case-level operation on exactly the endpoint that came back null.
- **THE PHASE 5 RESULT IS NEGATIVE, 2026-08-19: the ET Dice gain does NOT produce a better structured report.** Over 189 paired cases and 25 report-agreement metrics, Holm-corrected, `neurovision` vs `baseline_unet3d` has **exactly one** conclusive improvement (`relerr_vol_TC` +0.3843, p_holm 0.0215); `capacity_control` vs `baseline` has **none**. Structure-list Jaccard is 0.9074 / 0.9122 / 0.9108 across the three models, **16 of 25 metrics have the same median for all three**, and epicentre-structure match is nominally BETTER for the baseline (0.8783 vs 0.8571) — which rules out an underpowered-but-real effect. Patch size was controlled: all three trained and evaluated at 64³, verified from each run's own `eval_config.yaml`. The reason is mechanical: the report is dominated by *which structures the tumour overlaps*, and a few voxels at a margin rarely change whether a structure is involved at all. So the interpretable layer is **stable** with respect to segmentation quality across this range — good for deployment, fatal to a "better model gives a better report" claim. `docs/experiments.md` notes 22–25. **Do not write the "accuracy improves the report" framing.**
- **THE ELOQUENCE LAYER IS DEGENERATE ON BraTS 2021.** Over all 1251 cases: `near_eloquent` is True for **100%**, distance to the nearest Sawaya-listed structure is exactly 0.0 mm for **98.8%**, and 98.5% involve at least one eloquent structure. `agree_near_eloquent` and `agree_eloquent_any` are therefore constant at 1.0 with a CI of exactly [0, 0] in every comparison — no per-case information at all. Reporting "100% agreement on eloquence" would be true and completely misleading. `analysis/population.py` returns a `degenerate_fields` list and `scripts/population_stats.py` logs it at WARNING so the saturation cannot be mistaken for a result.
- **THE CAPACITY CONTROL LANDED 2026-08-17 AND THE ARCHITECTURAL CLAIM SURVIVES.** `capacity_control_unet3d` — a plain U-Net widened to 34,829,696 params, matched to `neurovision` within 0.23%, every other controlled variable verified identical to `baseline_unet3d` from the checkpoint's own stored config — trained 80/80 epochs and scores test Dice **ET 0.8497 / TC 0.9092 / WT 0.9295**. Paired over the same 189 cases: `neurovision` beats it by **+0.0211 ET (p_holm 7.3e-19)** and **+0.0068 TC (5.1e-07)**; widening alone buys **+0.0055 ET (1.6e-09)**. **So the +0.0267 ET gain decomposes as 20.6% capacity, 79.4% architecture** — the pre-registered "architecture" branch. Two qualifications that must go in the paper: capacity is NOT zero, so report the decomposition rather than claiming architecture alone; and **every WT comparison is inconclusive** (the bootstrap interval straddles zero at a saturated ~0.93), so no WT claim may be made from any of these runs. `docs/experiments.md` notes 19–21. Note the ET p_holm differs between tables (7.2e-22 in the three-way family, 1.4e-21 in the two-model one) purely because Holm is applied per family.

Two 1x1x1 probe convs (one per branch) predict region logits independently from DETACHED branch features; the gate is then fed `[cnn_feat, swin_proj, disagreement, H(p_cnn), H(p_swin)]`, where disagreement is `|p_cnn - p_swin|` and both entropies are Bernoulli entropies normalized to `[0, 1]` by `ln 2`. `3 * num_regions = 9` extra gate-input channels. `GateGenerator` gained `extra_channels` and raises in BOTH directions (ambiguity passed to a gate not built for it, or omitted from one that was). `AdaptiveGatedFusion` gained `use_ambiguity` / `num_regions` and `forward_with_branch_logits`; `FusionBlock` carries a default implementation returning `None`, so `ConcatFusion` / `AddFusion` satisfy the contract for free. `forward`'s existing `return_gate` contract is untouched — `forward_with_gates` and `scripts/extract_gates.py` depend on it
